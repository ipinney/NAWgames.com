Herbert Hoover

31st President of the United States

1929 -- 1933
Early Life
  • Born August 10, 1874 in West Branch, Iowa
  • First president born west of the Mississippi River!
  • Both parents died when he was young
  • Moved to Oregon to live with his uncle and aunt
  • He was a Quaker (a peaceful religion)
Hoover's birthplace in West Branch, Iowa
Herbert Hoover as Secretary of Commerce
Hoover with his dog King Tut at the White House
Education and Work
  • Worked his way through Stanford University
  • Studied geology (rocks and earth science!)
  • Became a mining engineer
  • Traveled the world -- worked in China and Australia!
  • By age 23 he was managing mines in Australia
Hoover Tower at Stanford University, named after him
Hero Before He Was President
  • During World War I, he helped feed millions of starving people in Europe
  • He led food rescue missions to Belgium, Germany, and Russia
  • People called him "The Great Humanitarian" because he helped so many people
  • In 1927, he led rescue and relief efforts after huge Mississippi River floods
  • He was nicknamed "Master of Emergencies"
As President
  • Became the 31st President in 1929
  • The Great Depression started -- many people lost jobs and money
  • He tried to help but the problems were very big
  • He signed the law that made "The Star-Spangled Banner" our national anthem!
  • Lost the 1932 election to Franklin D. Roosevelt
Cool Fun Facts
  • He gave away his entire presidential salary to charity -- he didn't keep a single penny!
  • He had a pet dog named King Tut at the White House
  • He and his wife could speak Mandarin Chinese and used it as a secret language!
  • He was the first Quaker to become president
  • He held the Florida fishing record for largest bonefish for 11 years!
  • He translated an old book about mining from Latin to English
The Hoover Dam, named after him!

What to Say -- Speaking Notes

Practice saying these out loud 3-4 times before your presentation. Try to remember the main ideas -- you don't have to say it word-for-word!

1 Introduction
Say something like:
"Hi! My president is Herbert Hoover. He was the 31st President of the United States. He was president from 1929 to 1933. Let me tell you some cool things about him!"
2 Early Life
Say something like:
"Herbert Hoover was born in 1874 in a small town in Iowa. He was the first president born west of the Mississippi River! Sadly, both of his parents died when he was little, so he moved to Oregon to live with his uncle. Even though life was hard, he worked really hard in school."
3 Education and Career
Say something like:
"Hoover went to Stanford University and studied rocks and earth science. He became a mining engineer and traveled all over the world! He even worked in China and Australia. By the time he was 23, he was already running mines in Australia!"
4 Helping People (The Best Part!)
Say something like:
"Before he was president, Hoover became famous for helping people. During World War I, millions of people in Europe were starving. Hoover organized huge food rescue missions and fed millions of people! People called him 'The Great Humanitarian.' That means someone who helps lots of people."
5 As President
Say something like:
"Hoover became president in 1929. But soon after, something really bad happened called the Great Depression. Lots of people lost their jobs and money. Hoover tried to help, but the problems were really big. One cool thing he did was sign the law that made 'The Star-Spangled Banner' our national anthem!"
6 Fun Facts and Closing
Say something like:
"Here are some fun facts: Hoover gave away ALL of his presidential salary to charity. He also had a dog named King Tut at the White House! And he and his wife could speak Chinese, and they used it as a secret language so nobody could understand them. Herbert Hoover lived to be 90 years old. Thank you for listening! Does anyone have questions?"

Tips to Get 25/25 on Every Category

  • Understanding of Content: Practice your speech enough times that you can talk about Hoover WITHOUT reading from your poster. Know the facts in your own words!
  • Posture and Eye Contact: Stand up straight and tall. Look at your classmates while you talk -- not at the floor or your poster. Pick 3 friends to look at and take turns looking at each one.
  • Oral Presentation: Speak slowly and clearly. Practice saying big words like "humanitarian" (hew-man-ih-TAIR-ee-un) and "engineer" (en-juh-NEER) out loud.
  • Audience Participation: When other kids present, sit still, look at them, and listen. No talking or wiggling! Be the best listener in the room.
  • Practice tip: Present to your family 3 times before the real thing. Have them sit like an audience and look at them while you talk!

What Your Teacher is Looking For (Rubric Reminder)

Understanding of Content (25 pts) Know your facts well enough to talk without reading from the poster
Posture and Eye Contact (25 pts) Stand up straight and look at the audience the whole time
Oral Presentation (25 pts) Speak clearly and pronounce all your words correctly
Audience Participation (25 pts) Listen quietly and pay attention when other kids present
