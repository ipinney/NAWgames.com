The board

A standard trifold

A 36 by 48 inch trifold: a 24 inch center panel and two 12 inch wings. On a 3 foot table the wings fold in a little more than usual so the whole board is 36 inches wide.

36 × 48 inBoard, open flat
12 | 24 | 12Left wing, center, right wing
3 inTallest title letters, readable across the room
9 rowsRubric, one row is the display

Board rules from St. Rose

  • Board text may be typed and printed: title, question, photos, data, graphs, and captions.
  • Every graph has a title and labels on both sides. Every photo has a caption.
  • The words are Addie's. The print-outs below are only the section titles.
  • The journal is separate, handwritten in cursive, and sits on the table.
Read order: judges read left to right, top to bottom. Put the question where the eye lands first, and the graphs at eye level in the middle.

Science steps, left to right

The table

Fitting it all on 3 feet

The laser is lined up for 3 feet, and the dot stays on the pod from 2 to 4 feet. At the fair the mosquito hangs about 2 feet from the camera, so the turret and the backdrop both fit across one table with the beam running sideways, never toward judges.

Top view, to scale for a 36 by 24 inch table. A deeper table works the same with more room behind the beam lane.

What changes for the fair

  • Small backdrop: a 12 by 12 inch piece of black foam board in the two printed feet. The big 30 by 20 inch board stays home for the experiments.
  • Short dowel: about 4 inches, so the mosquito hangs close to the small backdrop.
  • Tape marks: the turret's spot and the mosquito's spot, 2 feet apart, so setup takes a minute.
  • Fair pan limits: in the turret code, set the pan limits so the head cannot turn past the edges of the small backdrop. The tilt stop already keeps it low.
  • Demo: the judge pulls the mosquito back on the pendulum and lets go, or holds the wand from the front right corner. Nobody stands at the right end of the table or in the beam lane.
  • Front edge clear: nothing tall between the judges and the turret, so they can see the camera screen.
Before the first demo: arm switch off while setting up. Check that every shot ends on the black backdrop, with the mosquito pulled to each side of its swing.
Check with Miss Taggart: the table size, and whether the Parish Hall has an outlet or neighbors close on each side. The turret runs on its charged power bank and the wand on AAA batteries, so no outlet is needed. Charge the bank the night before: one charge runs about 4 to 6 hours.

Print-outs

Section titles, signs, and frames

Print on plain paper, cut on the dashed lines, and lay everything on the board before gluing anything. Addie's own text, photos, and graphs go under each title.

  1. Press Print. Choose Landscape and set scale to 100%, not Fit to Page.
  2. 12 pages come out: the title, the goal, the question, 4 sheets of section strips, the test labels, the laser sign, 2 graph frames, and a drawing frame.
  3. The graph and drawing frames are for practice or for Addie to fill in by hand. Printed graphs from the data are fine too.
