🖨️ Trifold board print-outs

Every section title for the board, one per page, at full size. Print them, cut on the dashed line, and glue them on the trifold in the exact layout the packet requires. The packet says board titles may be typed or printed, so these are allowed. The sketch still has to be hand drawn, so the last sheet is an empty frame to draw inside.

  1. Press Print. Choose Landscape and set scale to 100%, not Fit to Page.
  2. Nine pages come out. Cut each one on the dashed line.
  3. Left panel gets Purpose, the three small Purpose strips, Why, and How Does It Work. Center gets DUSTY and the sketch frame. Right gets Impact on Society, Catholic Connection, and Research Resources.
  4. Lay everything on the board before gluing anything down.
