How to read this

  • Every part has a primary and a backup. Buy the primary. Use the backup only if the primary is out of stock or late.
  • Primary uses four stores: DFRobot (the micro:bit, camera, board, and servos all come from one ecosystem), DigiKey (the laser and small parts), Adafruit (power cable, switch, and charge port), and Walmart (the power bank, also at Target and Micro Center).
  • Backups use Adafruit and DigiKey, plus DFRobot for the backup board and Target, Newark, or Mouser for the power parts.
  • Every link was opened on the dates above. Prices are what each site showed.
  • Amazon is left out on purpose: its listings change sellers and cannot be checked reliably.

Check before and after ordering

  • DigiKey prices marked * come from DigiKey's listing on Octopart; confirm in the cart.
  • DigiKey stock is thin on the 4-wire cable (16). Order it with everything else.
  • DFRobot ships from its US warehouse by UPS (3 to 7 days to Texas). Items not in the US warehouse ship from China in 10 to 17 business days with duties prepaid.
  • On arrival: check whether the Xia mi powers the micro:bit, and which 5 V port the servos run best on. With both servos moving and the laser on, the 5 V pins must read at least 4.6 V on the power bank; if not, feed the board's USB 5 V input instead. Measure the bank, the power switch, and the charge port before printing the base.
  • Laser: read the Class II label on the module before first power-up.

What the design locks in

  • Design A: pan and tilt camera turret, HuskyLens on top, laser below it, boresighted.
  • Printed tilt stop plus a code limit: cannot aim above the backdrop.
  • Laser path: 5 V port, through the arm switch, through the board relay, to the laser.
  • Ports: HuskyLens on the 5 V HuskyLens port; pan servo P1, tilt servo P2, speaker P0 (all 5 V); P8 spare.
  • Turret power: USB-C power bank on the base floor, bank to power switch to Xia mi DC jack; USB-C charge port in the right wall.
  • Wand: second micro:bit in a printed handle, 2xAAA pack, 4-wire cable down the line to the mosquito (sensor + LED eyes).
  • Fasteners: M2 everywhere in printed parts; M3 only for the HuskyLens.

If the primary board gives trouble

  • Switch to the micro:Driver (backup #02): servos on its servo headers, laser on motor channel M1, power from the same power bank (5 V is inside its 3.5 to 5.5 V range).
  • Its I2C port voltage is not documented. Test the HuskyLens on it before relying on it; if it browns out, power the HuskyLens from the bank's second (USB-C) port.
  • The printed base has room for either board (Xia mi 57 × 87 mm is the larger).
